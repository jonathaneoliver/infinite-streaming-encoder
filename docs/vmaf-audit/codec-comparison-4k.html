Reading it

the codec ordering is what you'd expect

AV1 > HEVC > H.264 in efficiency, and the gaps are large on this brutal high-motion FPV footage: AV1 needs less bitrate than H.264 and less than HEVC for the same VMAF. AV1's newer tools (larger blocks, better motion/transform coding) pay off most exactly where content is hard.

the gap widens with resolution

At 2160p, AV1 hits VMAF at where H.264 spends for a comparable score — the resolution rungs are where the modern codecs earn their keep.

caveats before you switch

AV1 encode is far slower than H.264/HEVC (watch fleet wall-time), and decode/playback support is narrower (older devices fall back to H.264). This is also one clip — BD-rate is content-dependent; validate on a corpus before committing a codec strategy.
