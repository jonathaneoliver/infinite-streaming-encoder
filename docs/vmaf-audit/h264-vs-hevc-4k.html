Reading it

what BD-rate means

Bjøntegaard-delta rate is the standard codec-comparison number: average % bitrate difference at equal quality, integrated across the overlapping VMAF range (on a log-bitrate axis). It's a fairer summary than any single rung, because it accounts for the whole curve.

why HEVC wins here

HEVC's larger coding units, better intra prediction, and — critically on this run — two-pass rate control let it place bits where the content needs them. On brutal high-motion FPV footage under a tight live VBV, that's worth roughly a fifth to a quarter of the bitrate. The gap widens with resolution: at 2160p HEVC needs 16.4 Mbps for VMAF 96.2 where H.264 spends 26.2 Mbps for 96.5 — ~37% off the top rung.
